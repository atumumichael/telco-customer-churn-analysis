# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company to identify the key factors associated with customer attrition and provide data-driven recommendations for improving customer retention.

The project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Program – Week 1: Business Analytics Case Study.

## Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer churn, which can lead to revenue loss and reduced customer lifetime value.

The main objective of this analysis is to understand:

- Why customers are leaving the company
- Which customer segments have higher churn
- Which services and contract types are associated with churn
- What actions the company can take to improve customer retention

## Dataset

The Telco Customer Churn dataset contains information about 7,043 customers across 21 variables.

Key variables include:

- Customer demographics
- Tenure
- Contract type
- Internet service
- Payment method
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Churn status

| Metric | Result |

| Total Customers | 7,043 |

| Churn Rate | 26.54% |
| Average Tenure | 2.37 |

## Key Findings

1. The overall customer churn rate is 26.54%.
2. Month-to-month customers show higher churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.
3. Customers using electronic check as their payment method show relatively high churn.
4. Fiber optic customers represent an important segment for further investigation.
5. Customers who churn tend to have higher average monthly charges.

## Business Recommendations

1. Encourage month-to-month customers to move to one-year or two-year contracts through targeted incentives.
2. Develop retention campaigns for customers showing signs of high churn risk.
3. Investigate service and pricing issues affecting Fiber Optic customers.
4. Encourage customers to use automatic payment methods.
5. Review pricing and service packages for customers with higher monthly charges.
